## Case Studies

### 01. Troubleshooting Under Time Pressure

This scenario demonstrates how I assess the situation, troubleshoot efficiently and focus on getting the user back to work.

### 02. Prioritizing Multiple Tickets

This scenario demonstrates how I evaluate priorities, consider SLAs and communicate with users while managing competing requests.

## How I Work With AI

I use AI to help me work more efficiently, without handing over the thinking or decision-making.

My preferred approach is interactive. I work through the scenarios myself, explain my reasoning and decide what actions to take. AI asks questions, provides feedback and helps me structure the final documentation.

These scenarios are based on situations and challenges I've encountered throughout my 14 years in IT support. They are illustrative examples, not copies of individual historical tickets.

The experience, reasoning and professional decisions behind them are my own.
